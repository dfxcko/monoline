Monoline

Design lamps, shades, vases and planters that print as one continuous line.

  1. Pick a piece. Lamps, shades for a lamp you own, vases, bowls and planters. New at the top brings the choice back.
  2. Shape it. Drag on the object to sculpt; drag empty space to turn it, pinch or scroll to zoom. Type exact sizes under Dimensions.
  3. Give it a surface. Choose patterns, fade one into another, paint them on, or open the wall with holes (Surface tab).
  4. Check it. The pill at the top left shows if it will print. Path shows the nozzle line, Beads the printed layers, Drawing a dimensioned section.
  5. Print it. Choose printer, nozzle and filament, then download G-code or STL (Print tab).
  6. Changed your mind? Undo steps back; the round arrow resets the piece to how it started. The arrow-up button makes a link to share it.
